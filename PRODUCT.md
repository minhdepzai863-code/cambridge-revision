# Cambridge Revision
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User selected Vercel and Supabase MCP. Buildless ES modules keep deployment and local preview independent of package installation. Supabase Auth, Postgres, and private Storage provide cloud persistence. IndexedDB provides an explicitly separate local workspace.

## Users
A Cambridge AS and A Level student revising STEM subjects during 2026–2027. Mathematics 9709, Physics 9702 and Computer Science 9618 are initial defaults; Further Mathematics 9231, Chemistry 9701 and Biology 9700 are optional. Subjects can be removed and re-added without erasing saved work.

## Product Purpose
Upload real papers, preserve repeat attempts, mark individual questions, correct mistakes, review weak topics, and measure actual progress.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Use official syllabus editions for selected exam year. Never invent marks, grades, exam dates, or Cambridge question provenance. Partial attempts never receive a full-paper grade. Component thresholds are separate from qualification grades. Retain every attempt unless explicitly deleted. Source must be published to the user's GitHub, followed by browser verification.

## Brand Commitments
The 6 November 2026 second design pass follows Apple minimalism, Linear precision, Attio workspace organization and Things simplicity, with Craft informing document presentation. Light mode is the primary identity: warm off-white canvas, white reading surfaces, charcoal text, silver dividers and restrained slate-blue charts. Use a neutral charcoal dark mode. Preserve the labelled sidebar and five core destinations. The redesign changes presentation only; preserve functionality, data structures, routes, authentication, uploads, attempts and backend connections. Motion may change to fit the visual system.

## Product Principles
Persistence before decoration. Measurements before confidence. Correct provenance before a grade. Next actions before clutter.

## Current appearance and complexity guidance

Performance, exam preparation and recent work lead the Overview. Subjects use compact management rows beneath them. Papers read as a document library with attachment controls and separate attempt history. Remove the notebook grid and decorative subject-cover shelf from the interface. Existing theme choices remain available; Light is the primary neutral identity. Secondary capabilities remain under Study tools. No new search, workflow, persistence or backend feature is part of this redesign.
