# Cambridge Revision
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User selected Vercel and Supabase MCP. Buildless ES modules keep deployment and local preview independent of package installation. Supabase Auth, Postgres, and private Storage provide cloud persistence. IndexedDB provides an explicitly separate local workspace.

## Users
A Cambridge AS and A Level student revising STEM subjects during 2026–2027. Mathematics 9709, Physics 9702 and Computer Science 9618 are initial defaults; Further Mathematics 9231, Chemistry 9701 and Biology 9700 are optional. Subjects can be removed and re-added without erasing saved work.

## Product Purpose
Upload real papers, preserve repeat attempts, mark individual questions, correct mistakes, review weak topics, and measure actual progress.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Use official syllabus editions for selected exam year. Never invent marks, grades, exam dates, or Cambridge question provenance. Partial attempts never receive a full-paper grade. Component thresholds are separate from qualification grades. Retain every attempt unless explicitly deleted. Source must be published to the user's GitHub, followed by browser verification.

## Brand Commitments
The revision workspace uses ivory, warm white and stone, with charcoal ink and a single muted slate-green accent. Dark mode uses neutral charcoal. Cambridge editorial typography, Apple restraint, Attio organization and PsychSAT workflow grouping inform the identity. The public folio uses credited archival library images and a labelled paper record. Keep the independent identity and preserve existing accounts, paper attempts, uploads and backend connections.

## Product Principles
Persistence before decoration. Measurements before confidence. Correct provenance before a grade. Next actions before clutter.

## Current appearance and complexity guidance

The dashboard leads with the next useful task and exam preparation, followed by actual marks and study activity. Subjects use compact management rows. The paper library groups attachments and repeat attempts and provides scoped search. Workspace, Practice, Review and Resources group destinations. Account preferences, planned sessions and bookmarks use the existing private settings records. The public landing and dedicated account pages sit apart from the cloud dashboard and the explicit local workspace.

## Academic collection presentation

Paper records are visually grouped by subject into folder-tab collections. Existing attachments and separate attempts remain library items with direct existing actions. Compact subject rows use charcoal/black symbol backplates. Grey folder geometry is an organisational cue for real paper/document collections; no filesystem, new Resources route or folder-upload mode was introduced. Preserve the performance-first Overview and practical labelled navigation.

## Academic editorial typography

The latest user direction supersedes the earlier Manrope-only constraint. Use Newsreader for major page and editorial section headings, and Open Sans for body text, navigation, controls, inputs, tables, scores, codes and analytics. The pairing evokes academic publishing while the tracker remains an independent tool; do not copy the University's logo, arms or official wordmark or imply affiliation. Six subject glyphs are distinct, monochrome and shown on the approved charcoal/black backplates.
