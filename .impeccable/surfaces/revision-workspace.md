# Revision workspace

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A student's working folio. Paper records, marks and corrections form an intelligible sequence across the public and private experiences.

**OWN-WORLD:** Ivory canvas, silver navigation, warm-white reading planes and charcoal ink. One muted slate-green marks selection and progress. Newsreader provides the editorial voice and Open Sans carries the evidence.

**STORY:** Discover the revision method, enter a private or local workspace, see the next useful task, and trace it back to a real paper, examination or saved question.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Public introduction sits left of a credited archival library photograph. A three-step strip leads into the labelled sample paper ledger, then an archival print and a closing action. The workspace opens with a compact welcome, a next action, an examination note and open metrics; the categorized sidebar follows the study cycle.

**FORM:** User-pinned Cambridge Ivory direction, grounded in the supplied workflow references. Code-led public preview and Operate workspace.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
