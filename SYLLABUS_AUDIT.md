# Syllabus and assessment audit

Reviewed 5 November 2026 against the official PDFs below. The app is a checklist of numbered topic headings and practical skill headings; the linked syllabuses remain the authority for individual learning outcomes, notes, exclusions, and examination rules.

- [Mathematics 9709, 2026–2027](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697427-2026-2027-syllabus.pdf): 38 numbered headings across Papers 1–6. AS routes are P1+P2, P1+P4, or P1+P5. A Level routes are P1+P3+P4+P5 or P1+P3+P5+P6. P1+P2 alone cannot be carried forward towards A Level. The app's route and marks/duration constants match the assessment overview. These constants describe the selected syllabus editions, not every historical paper; the paper cover takes precedence.
- [Physics 9702, 2025–2027](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/664565-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf): checked the numbered theory headings, added missing 11.2 Fundamental particles, and expanded practical skill breakdowns for Papers 3 and 5. The old app ID 9702:5.3 is retained as a supplementary focus within 5.1, rather than claiming a non-existent official section. Paper 3's raw marks are weighted by 0.75 for overall calculations.
- [Computer Science 9618, 2026](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697372-2026-syllabus.pdf) and [2027–2029 version 2](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/721397-2027-2029-syllabus.pdf): checked all 44 numbered headings. Paper 3 assesses sections 13–20; Paper 4 assesses sections 19–20 with the stated exclusions. The checklist now treats advanced algorithms and programming as relevant to both papers. Read the [2027 update notice](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/747147-2027-2029-syllabus-update.pdf) for level and calculator clarification.

Qualification estimates require the exact route, entry option, series, year, weighted maximum, and supplied threshold row. Carry-forward eligibility must be confirmed by the student's centre; the calculator cannot determine eligibility independently. Component indications never award A*.

## Optional STEM subjects

- [Further Mathematics 9231, 2026–2027](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697357-2026-2027-syllabus.pdf): all 24 numbered headings. AS routes P1+P3 or P1+P4; A Level P1–P4. P1/P2: 75 marks, 120 minutes; P3/P4: 50 marks, 90 minutes. P3 and P4 can be studied at either AS or A2.
- [Chemistry 9701, 2025–2027](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/664563-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf): 90 numbered theory headings (sections 1–22 AS, 23–37 A2), plus 19 practical headings and skill breakdowns for P3/P5.
- [Biology 9700, 2025–2027](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/664560-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf): 44 numbered theory headings (sections 1–11 AS, 12–19 A2), plus 18 practical headings and skill breakdowns for P3/P5.

Both sciences use P1/P2/P3 for AS and P1–P5 for A Level. Raw component maxima/durations match Physics; practical P3 is weighted by 0.75 for qualification estimates. Existing three subject defaults and topic IDs remain unchanged. Optional subjects are added to the workspace settings record and included in backups; legacy preferences without an explicit subject list also include subjects found in saved records. An explicit subject list supports removing any subject, including the defaults, without deleting its work.
